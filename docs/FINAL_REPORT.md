## 11. Remaining limitations

- **Early Blight and Potassium Deficiency stay data-limited.** 62 and 9 photographs in the whole dataset; their test support is 8 and 2 photos, so their per-class scores have very wide uncertainty.
- **Outlier exposure covers known unknowns.** The focused model learned to refuse the three dataset conditions it does not cover; a tomato disease it has never seen may still receive a diagnosis (see the PlantVillage rows above for how often this happens).
- **Domain shift.** All training photos come from one phone, two districts and leaves on paper. PlantVillage photos (another lab) and stress tests probe this, but field photos with complex backgrounds were not available.
- **Calibration of the ensemble** is fitted on single-network out-of-fold predictions and applied to the 5-network average; the test ECE above shows how well that transfers.
- **Test-set reuse.** The 290 test photos were reported in v1, v2 and v2.1 (never used for a choice). This round's choices were fixed on out-of-fold data with rules written before training; the test score above is a repeated look, not a first one.
- **Small test set.** Differences of a few points are within the bootstrap intervals; read the paired comparisons, not single numbers.
